2. Call recording and consent

Read this before switching on a phone number. Vaani answers calls and stores a transcript. In many jurisdictions recording or transcribing a call requires notifying the caller, and in some (including several US states and parts of the EU) it requires the consent of every party. India's DPDP Act requires notice and a lawful basis.

As the business deploying Vaani, you are the data controller for your calls and you are responsible for:

  • Announcing at the start of the call that it is handled by an automated assistant and may be transcribed
  • Confirming that this satisfies the consent rules everywhere you take calls
  • Setting a retention period appropriate to your industry
  • Honouring deletion requests from your callers

We provide the configurable greeting and retention controls to help you do this; we cannot determine your legal obligations for you.
